                trust boundary: which round is closed is the host's claim, not the library's finding. The
                aggregator cannot tell a genuine fix-and-rerun -- what this sample is staging -- from a host
                parking a failure in a round it simply never closes. Closing a round is an assertion you are
                accountable for, and the lifecycle log is where it is auditable.

What this run demonstrates: that the mechanism works end to end -- an Experience Run is
captured with both of its attempts, verified from host-supplied evidence, reflected on,
persisted, retrieved by a second run, injected as a labelled Historical Reference, and
that the exposure is recorded.

What it does not demonstrate: anything at all about model quality. The model here is a
scripted fixture with no credentials and no network, the clock and the identifiers are
fixtures too, and the reuse feedback claims no benefit because none was measured.
Measuring what reuse does to a real model is story 4.4's job, not this sample's.

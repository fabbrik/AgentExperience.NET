- (capability) CAP-1 through CAP-7 define the MVP contract: capture, deterministic evaluation, structured reflection, persistence/retrieval, MAF injection, lifecycle governance, and OpenTelemetry.
- (constraint) Core abstractions remain framework and storage independent; PostgreSQL/pgvector is the first production adapter; MAF integration captures failures; no private chain-of-thought is stored.
- (decision) The first vertical slice is a runnable MAF sample proving capture through reuse feedback, with deterministic evidence and automated scope/lifecycle tests.
- (event) Self-validation coherence pass: passed; all capabilities have intent and success, constraints affect design, non-goals are explicit, and success is demonstrable.
- (event) Self-validation preservation pass: passed; product brief and architecture claims needed by downstream implementation are represented in SPEC.md or mvp-design.md, with the architecture retained as an adopted companion.
- (decision) Accepted backlog critique fixes: final host-closed verification rounds bind evidence to artifact revisions; completion score is separate from confidence (1+S)/(2+S+F); host authorization bounds requested scope; Core finalization owns evaluation/reflection/policy/atomic persistence; indexing owns revision-checked embeddings with text fallback; trace IDs are excluded from metric labels; authorized retention/deletion erases live payloads with minimal tombstones. See epics.md stories 1.5, 2.1, 2.4-2.6, 3.1, 3.4, 4.1, 4.5; these supersede prior conflicting source proposals.
- (decision) Accepted upstream reuse boundaries: reuse MAF lifecycle/context middleware, IEmbeddingGenerator and proven vector/database connectors, evaluation and redaction adapters, and host telemetry/authorization. Keep domain verification, lifecycle, applicability and atomic persistence custom. Story 1.7 must prove compatibility; MagiCore (formerly Mem0Sharp) and AgentMemory.NET are optional integration candidates with substantial overlap. See specs/spec-agentexperience-net/reuse-boundaries.md.
